The problem0x001

The credentials are valid. That is the problem.

Your agent holds an API key, a card, a database — and an input box the outside world can write into. One poisoned document, and it does something expensive using credentials that are entirely legitimate. The API has no reason to refuse.

Afterwards the only record is the agent's own log. Identity products answer who is calling; the incident is about what the call was allowed to do.

Why teams are buying this year

  • Agents crossed into actions. Money moves, production systems get touched. The blast radius became financial, so containment became a purchase.
  • The regulation landed. Tamper-proof logging has been obligatory for EU financial entities since January 2025 (DORA RTS 2024/1774); auditors now ask for the agent trail by name.
  • Self-hosting is a requirement, not a taste. Teams with budget will not route agent traffic through another company's cloud — which rules out the managed-only field.
What the customer gets0x002

Damage has a ceiling

You set what each agent may spend, send and reach. The limits are enforced outside the agent, so one that has been talked into ignoring its instructions still cannot exceed them.

Every action has a receipt

A tamper-evident history per agent: what it called, on whose behalf, allowed or refused. Your auditor verifies it without trusting our word, our copy, or our keys.

Nothing leaves your side

One binary in your own infrastructure. If we disappeared tomorrow your deployment keeps running, on source your team can read line by line.

How it holds: every call is signed against its exact payload and the agent's current configuration, spending and network limits are enforced by a separate gateway process, and the audit trail is committed to Bitcoin and Solana so nobody can quietly rewrite it, us included.

What we do not claim

No certification and no independent audit report yet; HA is roadmap. SauronID contains an agent, it does not prove one is well-behaved — a limit set too generously still authorizes harm. Those tables ship with the code.

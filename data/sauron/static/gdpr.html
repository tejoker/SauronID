Retention Policy
GDPR applies to EU 27 + EEA (Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein) residents only. Users from China, USA, UK and other non-EU/EEA jurisdictions are exempt.
EU/EEA users who have not authenticated in the last 365 days are automatically anonymized under GDPR Art. 17 (Right to Erasure) and Art. 25 (Privacy by Design).
The following PII fields in users.csv are replaced with ANONYMIZED: first_name, last_name, email, address.
personas.csv is a read-only synthetic source of truth and is never modified.
Verification counts and behavioral analytics are retained (legitimate interest basis).
